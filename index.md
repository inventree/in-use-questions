# InvenTree In Use questionaire

Thank you for being interested in providing a use case / developer story for the InvenTree blog and socials.

## Process

1. Be interested in helping by sharing your experience with InvenTree - great, you are already on the right track!
2. Read this page and consider if you are willing to provide the required parts --> if you see a problem or have any concerns, feel free to reach out for clarification. The contact information is provided at the end of this page.
3. Prepare your answers to the questions listed below, giving us answers in the way that you would want to read them on the blog helps shortening the editing process
4. Ensure you are comfortable with the information you are providing, it will be posted publicly on the InvenTree blog and socials.
5. Ensure if you name an affiliated organization or company, you have the necessary permissions to do so.

### B. What is InvenTree solving for you?

*Readers relate most to the problem you started with.*

**8. What did you use before InvenTree? \***
Spreadsheets, another tool, an ERP, paper, nothing at all. This can be kept general, we do not want to bash other tools / vendors explicitly. It mostly helps to set the context for why you needed InvenTree and might help others that are in a similar situation.

**9. What problem made you look for something new?** *(optional)*

**10. Why did you choose InvenTree?** *(optional)*
For example open source, self-hosting, the API, cost, or community. This can be both your personal perspective or why your leadership or organsiation chose it.
